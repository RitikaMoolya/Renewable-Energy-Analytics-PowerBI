## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze renewable energy production trends over time
- Compare renewable energy performance across countries
- Evaluate installed capacity and capacity utilization
- Analyze renewable energy investments
- Measure renewable energy adoption and share
- Identify net energy exporters and importers
- Explore relationships between sustainability, innovation, and renewable production
- Present complex energy data through an interactive dashboard

---

## 🔍 Key Features

### ✨ Interactive KPI Cards
Quickly monitor important renewable energy performance metrics such as production, investment, installed capacity, capacity utilization, and renewable energy share.

### 📈 Trend Analysis
Analyze changes in renewable energy production over time and evaluate year-over-year growth.

### 🌍 Country-Level Analysis
Compare renewable energy production, capacity, investment, adoption, and other performance indicators across countries.

### ⚡ Energy Type Analysis
Compare renewable energy sources including:

- Biomass
- Geothermal
- Hydro
- Solar
- Wind

### 💰 Investment Analysis
Analyze renewable energy investment across countries and energy types.

### 🔄 Energy Trade Analysis
Identify countries that are net energy exporters or importers using energy export and import data.

### 🧠 Innovation Analysis
Explore the relationship between renewable energy and:

- Renewable Energy Patents
- Research Institutions
- R&D Expenditure
- Innovation Index
- Energy Storage

### 🌱 Sustainability Analysis
Explore relationships between renewable energy adoption, CO₂ emissions, energy storage, and other sustainability indicators.

### 🎛️ Interactive Slicers
Filter and explore the dashboard dynamically using:

- Year
- Country
- Energy Type

### 💬 Custom Report-Page Tooltip
A dedicated **Country Tooltip** provides a detailed renewable energy snapshot when users interact with country-level visuals.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file from the **PowerBI** folder.
2. Open the file using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the interactive slicers to filter the dashboard by:
   - Year
   - Country
   - Energy Type
4. Interact with the KPI cards and visualizations to explore the data.
5. Hover over supported country-level visuals to view the **Country Tooltip**.
6. Navigate between the dashboard pages using the page navigation buttons.
